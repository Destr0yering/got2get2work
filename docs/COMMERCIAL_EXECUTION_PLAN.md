# Got2Get2Work commercial execution plan

## Product boundary

Got2Get2Work is a user-focused, low-cost transportation coordination product. It helps people headed to the same destination at the same time discover and organize potential shared rides. Employers or community organizations may eventually sponsor access, but the primary experience and value proposition belong to the people coordinating transportation.

HoleFilled, formerly called ShiftSecure, is a separate employer-focused product. It responds when a worker calls out and creates a schedule vacancy. An AI-driven workflow identifies qualified potential replacements, and a voice agent contacts several candidates, explains the shift opportunity, and coordinates responses. HoleFilled must retain employer authorization, worker consent, qualification rules, calling-hour controls, escalation, opt-out, and human approval boundaries.

The two products must keep separate brands, repositories, customer claims, evidence, CRM pipelines, privacy notices, demos, and submission narratives. Got2Get2Work must not claim HoleFilled's shift-replacement automation, and HoleFilled must not be marketed as a coworker-carpooling product.

## Entry offer

For the current employer-sponsored validation path, sell a $199 early-adopter Shift Commute Resilience Assessment for one worksite and one difficult fixed-shift cohort. This is a channel and research offer supporting Got2Get2Work; it does not redefine the app as an employer scheduling or shift-replacement system. The buyer receives a 15-minute fit call, aggregate data checklist, privacy-safe Gemini-assisted analysis, written recommendations, and a findings call within 48 hours of receiving the agreed inputs.

## Ideal first contacts

Prioritize independent organizations where one missed commute can leave a shift uncovered and where a local decision-maker can approve a $199 assessment quickly:

1. Assisted-living and skilled-nursing facilities: administrator, director of nursing, HR director, staffing coordinator.
2. Warehouses and light manufacturing: site manager, operations manager, HR manager, workforce manager.
3. Hotels and contract hospitality: general manager, director of operations, HR manager.
4. Security and facilities contractors: branch manager, workforce operations director, scheduling manager.
5. Workforce nonprofits and staffing firms: executive director, employer-partnership lead, branch manager.

Exclude direct competitors, organizations without fixed shifts, and contacts whose roles cannot influence a site-level workforce pilot.

Required CRM fields: organization, website, contact name, role, work email, source, segment, worksite geography, reason qualified, current stage, next action, next-action date, consent/relationship notes, and evidence status. Do not store worker names, addresses, routes, schedules, health information, or personal Gmail data.

## Fifteen-minute pitch

**Opening:** Got2Get2Work helps fixed-shift employers understand and reduce commute-related shift risk without collecting employee home addresses or turning transportation data into employee surveillance.

**Discovery:** Which shifts are hardest to cover? What usually happens when transportation falls through? How do you estimate the operational cost? What aggregate information is already available? Who owns benefits, attendance operations, or workforce continuity?

**Offer:** For $199, we assess one site and one cohort using agreed aggregate facts. Gemini selects a bounded operational recommendation from approved actions and cites the aggregate facts used. A human reviews every recommendation; the system cannot contact workers, change eligibility, spend money, or promise transportation.

**Close:** If the assessment identifies a credible opportunity, propose a 21- to 60-day design-partner pilot. If it does not, the customer still receives a usable written risk assessment and recommendations.
