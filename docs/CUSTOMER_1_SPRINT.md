# Customer #1 sprint

Goal: close and deliver one arms-length $199 Shift Commute Resilience Assessment before the XPRIZE deadline.

## Offer

- Buyer: employer, staffing company, workforce nonprofit, or worksite operator.
- Scope: one worksite and one fixed-shift cohort.
- Price: $199 early-adopter fixed fee.
- Delivery: discovery call, data-minimized analysis of agreed aggregate inputs, written assessment, findings call.
- Delivery target: within 48 hours after discovery and receipt of the agreed aggregate inputs.
- Boundary: no live ride operation, driver certification, transportation guarantee, or employee-level surveillance.

Subject: 48-hour assessment for one difficult-to-cover shift

> I am launching Got2Get2Work, an employer-sponsored commute-resilience product for fixed-shift teams. I am offering a $199 early-adopter assessment for one worksite and one difficult shift cohort. You receive a discovery call, a data-minimized AI-assisted review of agreed aggregate inputs, a written set of operational recommendations, and a findings call with a 48-hour delivery target after discovery and receipt of those inputs. It does not require employee home addresses and does not operate transportation. Would a 15-minute fit call be useful this week?

## Integrity rules

- Do not count a founder transfer, investment, grant, unpaid promise, or reversible test payment as revenue.
- Disclose family, team-member, related-company, and pre-existing-customer revenue separately.
- Describe a deposit or preorder as such; do not call unearned money delivered-service revenue.
- Keep fictional demo figures out of customer evidence and the P&L.
