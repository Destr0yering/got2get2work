## Two acquisition motions

### Early-user adoption

Audience: hourly workers, shift workers, students, hospital staff, warehouse workers, and people commuting to the same campus or employment area.

Promise: coordinate a low-cost commute with people headed to the same destination at the same time. Do not promise guaranteed transportation, background checks, or employer monitoring.

Primary call to action: join the free Galaxy beta through `beta@got2get2work.com`.

Channels:

1. One local commute community or employment hub.
2. Samsung/Galaxy beta tester communities.
3. Local workforce-development and transportation-access groups.
4. Founder-led LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook community, and short demo posts.
5. Participant referrals after a completed or recovered commute.

### Partner validation

Audience: workforce-development organizations, community colleges, hospitals, warehouses, business parks, and transportation-demand-management programs.

Promise: a privacy-conscious way to help participants coordinate commutes and measure aggregate activation. Got2Get2Work remains user-directed; partners do not receive individual route or attendance surveillance.

Primary call to action: a 20-minute pilot-fit conversation through `partners@got2get2work.com`.

## Apollo prospecting specification

Create one Got2Get2Work list named `G2G2W — Pilot Partners — 2026`.

Target titles:

- Workforce Development Director
- Transportation Program Manager
- Employee Experience or Benefits Manager
- Community Partnerships Director
- Student Services or Basic Needs Director
- Sustainability or Transportation Demand Manager

Target organizations:

- 100–5,000 employees or participants
- Fixed worksites or campuses
- Early, late, weekend, or variable shifts
- Locations where public transit coverage is limited outside peak hours
- Initial geography limited to one launch region

Exclude staffing agencies selling shift replacement, scheduling automation buyers, and HoleFilled/ShiftSecure prospects.

## HubSpot structure

Keep the default HubSpot account, but remove or archive sample records before real reporting.

Recommended lifecycle:

`Prospect identified → Contacted → Replied → Discovery scheduled → Beta partner → Active pilot → Closed/lost`

Minimum company fields: name, domain, industry, employee/participant range, region, commute pain hypothesis, Apollo source date.

Minimum contact fields: first name, last name, work email, title, company, outreach status, last contact date, consent/source note.

Never import personal email addresses merely because Apollo exposes them. Prefer verified work emails and respect opt-outs.

## 30-day launch sequence

### Week 1 — proof and recruitment

- Finish installable Galaxy test APK and production authentication.
- Recruit 10–25 controlled testers from one geographic community.
- Publish one 45–60 second product demonstration.
- Create the Apollo partner list and qualify the first 25 organizations.

### Week 2 — activation

- Onboard testers personally.
- Measure profile saved, schedule confirmed, match viewed, request sent, mutual acceptance, and recovery.
- Contact no more than five carefully researched partners per working day.

### Week 3 — evidence

- Fix the three largest activation failures.
- Collect permissioned user quotes and aggregate funnel evidence.
- Publish a build-in-public/XPRIZE evidence update.

### Week 4 — conversion

- Invite responsive organizations into a small pilot.
- Prepare the Galaxy Store closed-beta listing.
- Convert verified evidence into the XPRIZE submission narrative and demo.

## Email templates

### Beta invitation

Subject: Help test a simpler way to get to work

Got2Get2Work helps people coordinate with others headed to the same destination at the same time. We are inviting a small group of Samsung Galaxy users to test the free beta. The test focuses on profile setup, schedule review, matching, ride coordination, and recovery when a plan changes. Interested? Reply with your Galaxy model and general commute area—no home address required.

### Partner introduction

Subject: A small commute-access pilot for [organization]

Hi [first name], I’m building Got2Get2Work, a user-directed carpool coordination app for people headed to the same workplace or campus at the same time. We are preparing a small Galaxy beta and looking for one launch community where off-peak commute access is a recurring problem. The product uses approximate areas, mutual acceptance, and aggregate pilot reporting rather than employee-level route or attendance surveillance. Would a 20-minute pilot-fit conversation be useful?

### Press/hackathon note

Subject: Got2Get2Work turns schedule overlap into practical commute coordination

Got2Get2Work is a people-first carpool coordination app being prepared for a Samsung Galaxy beta and an XPRIZE hackathon submission. It helps users organize around destination and time overlap, includes consent and recovery workflows, and uses live Gemini operations with deterministic safety boundaries. We can share a short demo and verified build evidence.
